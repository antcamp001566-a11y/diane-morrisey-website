> **The key intent conversation with Diane:** with ~2M followers, ad networks will pitch her. That's the biggest fork in this business. Option A is maximum ad revenue, which means the cluttered pages her followers complain about elsewhere (pop-ups and autoplay with sound rank among the least-liked mobile ad formats, Coalition for Better Ads via [Market dept.](../departments/diane-market.md)). Option B is a fast, clean site funded mainly by brand work, classes and events, possibly with light ads later. This document recommends **B**.

## Pricing philosophy

1. **Recipes are free, forever.** They're what built her audience. Sponsored recipes stay free too.
2. **Charge for time saved, time together, and depth**: meal plans, classes, events, recipe development.
3. **Price from value**, benchmarked against comparable offers (see the benchmark table in the [Revenue dept. report](../departments/diane-revenue.md)).
4. **One new paid offer at a time.** Launch order: disclosure policy + rate card (R1, R2) → first class (D6) → corporate pilot (D8) → membership (D9), only after classes show repeat buyers.
5. **No dark patterns in billing:** one-click cancel, renewal reminders, no fake scarcity.
