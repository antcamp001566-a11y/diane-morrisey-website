# 00 — The Intent Standard

> **AI-assisted document.** Drafted with Claude (AI) as an evaluation lens. Per MK-320 rules, the team develops its recommendations first, then uses this to check them. Label any part used in the deck or action plan as AI-assisted.

Every other document in this folder is written against this standard. It exists because the most common way a food creator's website goes wrong isn't malice. It's **intent drift**. Recipe sites are the textbook example: the recipe gets buried under a long story, autoplay video and dozens of ads, because every scroll earns ad money. Nobody decided to make the reader's life worse. The site was simply built to answer "how do we earn from traffic?" instead of "how do we help someone get dinner on the table?"

Diane's brand is literally **"You Got This!"**: confidence for home cooks. The website should feel like that promise, not undercut it.

---

## The Intent Spectrum

| Level | Name | The question it started from | Role of the home cook | Role of revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Revenue-first** | "How do we monetize 2M followers?" | Traffic to sell | The purpose |
| 2 | **Revenue-led** | "How do we turn followers into buyers?" | A market | Primary goal; recipes are the means |
| 3 | **Exchange** | "What can Diane offer at a fair price?" | Trading partner | Equal to value |
| 4 | **Value-led** | "What do her cooks need that Diane can sustainably provide?" | The reason | Viability check |
| 5 | **Value-first** | "What helps someone confidently get a meal on the table, and come back tomorrow?" | The point | Fuel and byproduct |

**Target: every recommendation sits at level 4 or 5.** Revenue isn't forbidden. With an audience this size, real money is available. Revenue is a *constraint the idea has to meet*, not *the reason the idea exists*.

## Why intent matters even when nothing harmful happens

1. **What gets measured.** A revenue-first recipe site tracks page views and ad revenue per session, which rewards *longer* pages and *more* scrolling. A value-first site tracks "I made this," saves, prints and returns, which rewards *faster* paths to the recipe.
2. **Where the investment goes.** Revenue-first effort goes into ad slots, pop-ups and SEO padding. Value-first effort goes into tested recipes, clear steps, a good search and a printable card.
3. **What improves over time.** You get good at whatever you optimize.

## Diagnosing intent

| Signal | Value-first (4–5) | Revenue-first (1–2) |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin trace** | Something cooks asked for (DMs, comments, Phase 1 interviews) | A monetization playbook or a revenue target |
| **Success metric** | Recipes made, saved, printed; return visits | Page views, ad RPM, conversions only |
| **Grammar test** | "Help a tired parent find a 30-minute dinner" | "Get followers to click through and buy" |
| **Swap test** | Diane picks the version that helps cooks and earns less | Diane picks the version that earns more and helps less |
| **Investment destination** | Recipes, usability, relationship | Ad and conversion mechanics |

## Re-rooting for Diane

| Revenue-first | Re-rooted |
|---|---|
| "Put ads on the recipe pages. 2M followers is a lot of ad money." | "What does a cook need on a recipe page?" A *Jump to Recipe* button, a printable card and a fast page. If ads come later, they never sit between the cook and the recipe. |
| "Gate recipes behind an email sign-up" | "What would cooks want delivered?" A free weekly "what's for dinner" email they *choose* to get. |
| "Sell a second cookbook" | "What do followers keep asking her for that book #1 didn't cover?" |
| "Take every brand deal offered" | "Which products does Diane actually use?" Her Whole Foods credibility is the asset, so partner only where it's true, and disclose it. |

## The rule for every recommendation

1. **Label** its intent level.
2. **Re-root** anything at level 1–2 before Diane sees it.
3. **Name the value investment**: what goes into the thing itself.
4. **Then** show the revenue math, as proof that the value-first version is viable.

> **"Revenue is how you'll know it worked, not what you're trying to do."**

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
