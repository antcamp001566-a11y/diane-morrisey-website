# 02 — Mission, Vision, Values & Pitches

> **AI-assisted document.** All statements here are `[Suggested]` templates. Bracketed text should come from Phase 1 findings and from Diane. A mission written for someone without their input is a draft, not a mission.

## Mission `[Suggested]`

> **To share [Diane's work / story / expertise] in a way that genuinely helps [her audience], and to give them a place worth coming back to.**

Why this shape: it names *who benefits* and *what they get* before anything about Diane's goals. **`Intent: 5`**

## Vision `[Suggested]`

> **A site that becomes the trusted home for [audience] who care about [topic], and grows because people tell each other about it, not because it chases them.**

## Values `[Suggested]`

Each value includes a **decision test** so it actually guides choices.

| Value | What it means | Decision test |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience first** | Every page exists because someone needs it | "Which Phase 1 interviewee would use this?" No answer means don't build it. |
| **Real over polished** | Diane's own voice and story beat generic professional copy | "Could this sentence appear on anyone's site?" If yes, rewrite it. |
| **Earn the return visit** | Success means people come back by choice | "Would they come back if we removed every reminder and pop-up?" |
| **Honest exchange** | Anything that costs the visitor money, data or attention says so up front | "Would Diane be comfortable if a visitor read why we added this?" |
| **Sustainable pace** | Publish only on a schedule Diane can keep for a year | "Can she still do this in month 12?" |

## Elevator pitches `[Suggested]`

**10 seconds**
> "DianeMorrisey.com [placeholder domain] is where [audience] goes for [core value], straight from Diane."

**30 seconds**
> "Diane's [work/story] already matters to [audience]. Right now it's [scattered / hard to find / only shared in person — Phase 1]. The site puts it in one place, with [core content] people come back for every [week/month], and an easy way to stay in touch or work with her."

**One paragraph**
> "Diane Morrisey's website exists for the people who already value what she does and the people who are about to discover it. Our Phase 1 interviews showed [key finding — cite participant + time stamp]. The site answers that with [content focus], published on a rhythm Diane can sustain, plus a simple way to stay connected. Success isn't measured by traffic spikes. It's measured by how many people come back, reply and pass it on. Anything that earns money later grows out of that trust, not the other way around."
