# 02 — Mission, Vision, Values & Pitches

> **AI-assisted document.** All statements are `[Suggested]` drafts built on Diane's public brand ("You Got This!"). Confirm them with Diane; a mission written for someone is a draft until she owns it.

## Mission `[Suggested]`

> **To give every home cook the confidence to put a great meal on the table, with recipes anyone can make and everyone will love.**

It echoes her cookbook's own subtitle, so it's her promise, not a new one. **`Intent: 5`**

## Vision `[Suggested]`

> **To be the place families turn to every night when they ask "what's for dinner?", trusted because every recipe works, and grown because cooks tell other cooks.**

## Values `[Suggested]`

| Value | What it means | Decision test |
|---|---|---|
| **You got this** | Every recipe and page should build the cook's confidence | "Would a nervous beginner feel capable after reading this?" |
| **Get to the food** | Respect the cook's time | "How many scrolls to the ingredient list?" (target: one tap) |
| **Tested at a real table** | Recipes feed a real family of eight first | "Has Diane made this at home?" |
| **Honest kitchen** | Partnerships only for products Diane uses, always disclosed | "Would Diane recommend this if she weren't paid?" |
| **Everyone eats** | Options for carnivores to vegetarians, big families to empty-nesters | "Is there a swap or scale note?" |

## Elevator pitches `[Suggested]`

**10 seconds**
> "Diane's 2 million followers love her recipes, but they're stuck in Instagram captions. The website gives every recipe a home you can search, save and print."

**30 seconds**
> "Diane Morrisey taught herself to cook as a young mom of six, ran prepared foods for Whole Foods, and now shares easy family recipes with about 2 million followers. The website fixes that: a searchable recipe library, a free weekly 'What's for Dinner' email, and a home for her cookbooks and classes, all built around her promise: *You Got This!*"
