# 06 — Core Standard Operating Procedures

> **AI-assisted document.** All SOPs are `[Suggested]`. They're sized for **one person (Diane) with limited time**. If an SOP can't be kept up in month 12, simplify it rather than drop it.

---

## SOP 1: Audience acquisition (bringing new people in)

**Principle:** people come because someone they trust pointed them there, not because they were chased.

| Step | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Share each new content piece on the 1–2 platforms where the audience already is `[Phase 1]` | Each publish |
| 2 | Add the site link to email signature, social bios and any organization profiles | Once |
| 3 | When something resonates, ask: "Know someone this would help? Pass it on." | Each newsletter |
| 4 | Make sure searching "Diane Morrisey" shows the site (Google Business Profile or Search Console, plus a clear page title) | Once, then quarterly |
| 5 | Mention the site naturally in talks and conversations | Ongoing |

**Not in this SOP:** buying followers, pop-ups on arrival, paid ads before the content core proves itself.

## SOP 2: Onboarding (first visit → returning visitor)

| Step | What happens | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The visitor understands who Diane is and what's here within 5 seconds | Test it on someone who's never seen the site |
| 2 | They find one piece of content worth their time | Home page features 2–3 "start here" pieces |
| 3 | *After* that content, a quiet invitation: "Want the next one? Here's how." | No pop-up before they've read anything |
| 4 | New subscribers get **one** welcome email: who Diane is, what to expect, how often, and the best 3 pieces | Set up once |
| 5 | Tell them how to unsubscribe, plainly | In every email |

## SOP 3: Content delivery (the core product)

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Source:** keep a running list of questions and topics from readers, conversations and Phase 1 themes |
| 2 | **Choose:** each cycle, pick the item the audience most needs, not the one most likely to go viral |
| 3 | **Create:** in Diane's voice (doc 03). Edit for clarity, not polish. |
| 4 | **Intent check:** "Who is this for, and what do they get?" (doc 00) |
| 5 | **Publish** on the committed rhythm. Consistency beats volume. |
| 6 | **Distribute** via SOP 1 and the newsletter |
| 7 | **Learn:** monthly, review returning visitors, replies and the top 3 pieces, and adjust topics |

**Bad-month rule:** if Diane can't create, she shares something from the archive with a short new note. The rhythm continues.

## SOP 4: Support & inquiries

| Inquiry type | Response target | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Reader message or reply | Within 1 week | Personal reply. These are the most valuable signals the site gets. Log recurring questions into the SOP 3 topic list. |
| Professional inquiry | Within 2 business days | Acknowledge, ask 2–3 qualifying questions, then follow up `[Ask Diane]` on rates and availability |
| Technical issue (broken link or page) | Within 1 week | Fix, or note for whoever maintains the site |
| Unsubscribe / data request | Immediately | Honor without friction |

## SOP 5: Monthly intent review (15 minutes)

1. Are returning visitors stable or growing?
2. Did anything we added this month start from a revenue question instead of an audience question?
3. Is there anything we'd be uncomfortable explaining to a reader?
4. Is the publishing rhythm still sustainable?

If question 2 or 3 gets a "yes," re-root it (doc 00).
