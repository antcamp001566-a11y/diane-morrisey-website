# 06 — Core Standard Operating Procedures

> **AI-assisted document.** All SOPs are `[Suggested]`, sized for Diane plus, at most, one part-time helper.

---

## SOP 1: Audience acquisition (Instagram → site)

Diane doesn't need to find an audience. She needs to **bring the one she has home**.

| Step | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Publish the recipe card on the site **before** the Reel goes live | Each recipe |
| 2 | Reel caption: short hook + "Full recipe at [site] (link in bio)." Keep the short recipe in the caption for now so nobody feels baited. | Each Reel |
| 3 | Link in bio → a page listing the latest recipes, or a "recipe from today's Reel" link | Always |
| 4 | Story sticker linking to the new recipe | Each Reel |
| 5 | Share to the Facebook page | Each recipe |
| 6 | Pinterest pin per recipe `[Suggested]`. Recipe traffic on Pinterest lasts for years. | Each recipe |
| 7 | Recipe schema checked in Google's Rich Results Test | Each recipe |

## SOP 2: Onboarding (first visit → returning cook)

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Arrives from Instagram → lands **directly on the recipe**, not the home page |
| 2 | Cooks it. Print button and cook mode work. |
| 3 | Below the recipe card: "Want a dinner idea every week? Join What's for Dinner." No pop-up before they've seen the recipe. |
| 4 | Welcome email: Diane's story in 3 lines, her 5 most-loved recipes, what to expect, and how often |
| 5 | Email #2 (one week later): "Did you make anything? Hit reply and tell me." Replies feed SOP 3. |

## SOP 3: Content delivery

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Source:** log follower questions, DM requests, comments and site searches that return no results |
| 2 | **Plan:** each week, pick recipes for what cooks need now (season, holidays, requests) |
| 3 | **Cook and shoot:** as she does today for Instagram |
| 4 | **Write the card:** ingredients, steps, time, servings, swaps, scaling note. Story goes below the card or stays short. |
| 5 | **Intent check:** "Can a nervous beginner make this from the card alone?" |
| 6 | **Publish → Reel → newsletter** (SOP 1 order) |
| 7 | **Monthly review:** top recipes, failed searches, reader replies → next month's plan |

**Back-catalog migration** (one-time project): order by Instagram saves; target 10–20 recipes a week; tag time, meal, protein and diet consistently.

## SOP 4: Support & inquiries

| Type | Target | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| "My recipe didn't work" | 3 days | Reply personally; fix the card if it's unclear. A failed recipe breaks the "You Got This!" promise. |
| Reader replies and "I made it!" | 1 week | Reply or like; ask permission to share as social proof |
| Brand / media / event inquiry | 2 business days | Send the media kit; qualify fit ("Would Diane use this anyway?"); route rates to Diane `[Ask Diane]` |
| Broken page or link | 1 week | Fix, or log for the site maintainer |
| Unsubscribe or data request | Immediately | Honor with no friction |

## SOP 5: Monthly intent review (15 minutes)

1. Returning visitors, saves and "I made it" replies: up or down?
2. How many scrolls to reach the recipe on a phone? (It should never grow.)
3. Did we add anything this month that started from a revenue question?
4. Would Diane be comfortable if a follower saw why we added it?

A "yes" on question 3 or a "no" on question 4 → re-root it (doc 00).
